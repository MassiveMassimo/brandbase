# Apple

## Logo (required)
- Official name: Apple
- Default file: apple-24.svg (black Apple logomark — the bitten apple icon used since 1998)

## Colors
- Primary: #000000 (black — Apple's primary brand color for logo usage)
- Secondary: #ffffff (white — used on dark backgrounds)
- Background restrictions: The Apple logo should appear in black on light backgrounds and white on dark backgrounds. Apple does not authorize colored versions of the logo for third-party use.

## Usage
- Do not: Modify, distort, or add visual effects to the Apple logo. Do not use the Apple logo for commercial purposes without prior written consent. Do not use the Apple logo as the most prominent element in third-party materials. Do not create your own versions or lockups using the Apple logo.
- The Apple logo (Option-Shift-K) is a registered trademark. Unauthorized commercial use may constitute trademark infringement.

## Variants
- Dark mode notes: apple-24-light.svg (white Apple logomark for dark backgrounds)

## Missing Assets
Official assets that exist but are not included because they are not available in SVG format:
- Apple wordmark (logotype): The "Apple" text logotype is not publicly distributed as a standalone SVG. Apple's identity primarily uses the logomark alone.
