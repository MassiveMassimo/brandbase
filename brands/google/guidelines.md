# Google

## Logo (required)
- Official name: Google
- Default file: google-24.svg (multicolor "G" logomark — the standard Google favicon/icon)

## Colors
- Blue: #4285F4
- Red: #EA4335
- Yellow: #FBBC05
- Green: #34A853
- Background restrictions: Prefer full-color logo on white background. Grey or white versions acceptable when there are constraints.

## Clear Space
- Minimum clear space: Equal to or greater than the height of the "G" in the Google logo.

## Usage
- Do not: Modify or distort the logo, change any colors, add additional elements, crowd the logo with images/text/graphics, use the logo as the most prominent element, imply affiliation or endorsement where none exists.

## Variants
- Dark mode notes: google-24-light.svg (white "G" logomark for dark backgrounds)
- Wordmark notes: google-wordmark.svg (full-color wordmark, 272x92 native aspect ratio), google-wordmark-light.svg (white wordmark), google-wordmark-monochrome.svg (black wordmark)
